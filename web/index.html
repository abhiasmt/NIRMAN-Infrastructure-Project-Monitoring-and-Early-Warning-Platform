From Reactive Reporting to Proactive Intervention

Periodic returns tell an officer what has already happened. The harder question — which project will slip next, and why — is left to manual judgement across hundreds of records.

Late Identification

Delays and cost escalations may be visible only after major project slippage.

Manual Prioritisation

Officers may need to review many project records to identify urgent cases.

Limited Root-Cause Visibility

A status report may show a delay but not the data-backed factors driving future risk.

A Risk Score Is Not Enough. NIRMAN Explains Why.

A number on its own cannot be acted on or defended in a review meeting. Every prediction carries the project-specific factors that produced it and a suggested focus for the officer's review — so the officer, not the model, decides what happens next.

Scores are presented as risk probability and early-warning indicators. They are decision support, not a forecast of a certain outcome.

Designed for Smarter Infrastructure Governance

What the platform is built to change in the monitoring cycle.

Earlier Risk Identification

Risk indicators surface as returns arrive, rather than after visible slippage.

Focused Project Reviews

Review effort concentrates on the projects ranked highest by risk score.

Transparent Decision Support

Each score carries its drivers, so recommendations can be examined and challenged.

Timely Infrastructure Delivery

Interventions are assigned, tracked and closed within the same monitoring loop.

Built for Secure, Scalable Decision Support

A service-separated architecture: the model never talks to the browser directly, and the data layer stays behind an authenticated API with role-based access control.
